Difficulty Settings — Red Tape Simulator (Revised)

🏘️ "Small Town Government" (Easy)
"Everybody knows everybody. The rules are more like guidelines."
Player aids active:

Document checklist visible on every application — lists exactly what's required and which documents qualify
Forged/inconsistent documents have a subtle visual indicator
Bribe attempts are obvious and clearly telegraphed
Grace period on SLA timers
End-of-shift summary explains every mistake and why it was wrong


🏢 "County Seat" (Medium)
"There's a handbook. You were supposed to read it."
Player aids active:

Document checklist visible but shows category names only — you know you need "Proof of Residence" but must know which documents qualify
No visual indicator on forged documents — judge by content
Bribe attempts are naturalistic; easy to miss if distracted
Standard SLA timers
End-of-shift summary shows what you got wrong, but not why


🏛️ "State Capital" (Hard)
"The rulebook was updated last Tuesday. No, we didn't send a memo."
Player aids: None
Additional friction:

No document checklist — requirements are yours to memorize
Citizens occasionally give wrong information confidently — you have to catch it against their documents
Bribe attempts can be disguised as innocent gestures
Tighter SLA timers create real tension between speed and accuracy


🔥 "DOGE Is Watching" (Nightmare)
"Your position has been flagged for efficiency review. Please justify your existence by Friday."
Everything from State Capital, plus:

Audit events — random shifts trigger a silent audit; a stranger sits in the corner taking notes and you don't know which decisions they're watching
Staffing cuts — occasional shifts where you handle 2x the citizen volume with a memo that your colleague "has been let go"
No end-of-shift feedback — performance score shown as a single number, no breakdown
Morale meter — extended poor performance triggers increasingly bleak desk commentary from your character; bottoms out to a "voluntary resignation" game over


The difficulty selection form framing stays the same — that presentation is too good to cut.
